Website Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Purpose of Policy

Healthline Pharmacy ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how personal information collected through our website is handled. This template policy applies solely to information collected through this website and does not substitute for clinical patient privacy policies mandated by relevant healthcare regulatory bodies.

2. Important Medical Information Safeguard

Users should avoid submitting sensitive medical history, personal health numbers, or active prescription details through general website contact forms. Unencrypted website communication is not guaranteed to be secure. Please contact our pharmacy directly by phone at 905-458-9000 for clinical inquiries.

3. Information Collected

When using our website, we may collect non-personal analytics information (such as browser type, operating system, and pages visited) along with information voluntarily submitted through our general contact form (such as name, phone number, and general inquiry topic).

4. Use of Information

Voluntarily submitted contact information is used exclusively to respond to your direct inquiries or provide requested information regarding pharmacy hours, service availability, or directions.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies and basic performance analytics tools to optimize website speed, responsive layout rendering, and accessibility. You can configure your web browser to disable or decline cookies if preferred.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to external platforms, such as Google Maps for directions. We do not control or accept responsibility for the privacy practices of external third-party websites.
